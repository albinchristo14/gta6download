**A message offering a free GTA 6 key is a claim, not proof that you have won a game.** This guide helps you evaluate a giveaway; it does not offer keys or run a promotion. Rockstar lists paid pre-orders for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in its [official pre-order FAQ](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/46YkMi4rHxYXMeIHtZiKaE/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-order-details). That does not rule out a legitimate future promotion, but it does not validate an unsolicited offer either.

## Check the giveaway before responding

- Find the promotion independently on the organizer's verified website or official platform account. A copied logo or a familiar-looking username can be impersonated.
- Look for published terms: organizer, eligibility, closing date, prize, selection method and how winners are contacted. Missing terms or a mismatched domain are reasons to pause.
- Check what the prize actually is. A subscription trial, discount or in-game bonus is not necessarily a copy of GTA 6. Confirm the console and account region before redeeming a code.
- Never give someone your password or one-time sign-in code. Treat requests to install software, disable protection or pay an unexpected release fee as warning signs.
- Be cautious of urgency, unsolicited direct messages and links that ask you to sign in. Navigate to the official site yourself. The FTC's [phishing guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams) explains how to inspect suspicious messages.

A real-looking code, screenshot or winner list does not independently prove an offer is genuine. Do not forward suspicious links to friends as a condition of claiming a prize.

## If you already responded

If you entered a password, change it from a trusted device, sign out other sessions and enable two-step verification on the affected account. If you reused the password, change it elsewhere too. Contact the relevant platform's support if you cannot access your account. If you gave payment information, contact your card issuer or payment provider promptly. If you installed an unknown file, disconnect the affected device and seek trusted technical assistance.
