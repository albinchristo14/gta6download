## A payment is not a location

Money leaving your account proves that an order exists somewhere. It does not prove which account, which store region or which console the resulting copy belongs to, and almost every "my pre-order has vanished" report is one of those three mismatches rather than a lost purchase. Keep that framing, because it survives contact with the actual fix.

It also matters that nothing is downloadable yet. GTA 6 releases on 19 November 2026, so a queue or library with no GTA 6 entry in it today is behaving correctly. If a site did take payment and hand over a file, a licence key for immediate use, a PC installer or an app package, that was never a copy of the game; treat it as malware and work through [how to avoid GTA 6 download scams](/news/how-to-avoid-gta-6-download-scams) before you touch your console sign-in details again.

## The usual places a pre-order turns up

| What you are seeing | What is normally going on |
| --- | --- |
| A grey or locked tile, no download | The entitlement belongs to another account on the same console |
| No entry anywhere in the library | You are browsing a different store region from the one you bought in |
| The retailer shows the order, the console does not | A delivered code has not been redeemed, or was redeemed on a profile you are not using |
| No order on either side | The retailer authorisation is still pending, or the order failed after checkout |

## Codes, bundles and second accounts

Digital codes bought separately from a storefront are the most common source of confusion, because purchasing and entitlement are two distinct events: the receipt proves the first, the library only reflects the second. A code redeemed once is spent, and it is spent on whichever account completed redemption, which is why a resale purchase can arrive already used. Bundle and subscription entitlements add another account layer of their own.

## Before launch night, not during it

Run the boring checks while there is still time. Sign into the purchasing account on the console you intend to play on, and confirm the copy appears in that account's library months in advance; if it is invisible now, it will not materialise on 19 November 2026. Note the store region printed on the order.
