Grand Theft Auto VI releases on **November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That date is set by Rockstar's own Newswire and is the date printed on the game's official product pages as of September 2026 — and no PC version has been announced at all.

## What Rockstar's own pages say right now

The official Grand Theft Auto VI site lists two launch platforms — PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — with a November 19, 2026 release, and describes a story about **Jason and Lucia** running across the state of **Leonida**, the wider map that already contains Vice City. Those are Rockstar's own words on its own product page, which is the only listing on this topic worth treating as authoritative.

Nothing in that listing mentions Windows. Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA VI, has not given a PC date, and has not published PC system requirements. That silence is a fact about the situation, not a gap in our reporting, and we treat it as such on our [system requirements page](/gta-6-system-requirements).

## How the date moved: three windows, two slips

Understanding why nobody should treat any Rockstar date as sacred requires the history:

1. **Announced for 2025.** Rockstar revealed GTA VI with a trailer in early December 2023, and Take-Two guided to a 2025 launch window.
2. **Moved to May 26, 2026.** In May 2025 Rockstar reset expectations with a Newswire post titled "Grand Theft Auto VI is Now Coming May 26, 2026," pushing the game roughly seven months later than the original window's midpoint.
3. **Moved again to November 19, 2026.** On 6 November 2025, Rockstar announced a second delay. BBC News reported the company's stated reason was that the extra months were needed to reach the level of **polish** fans expect and deserve — the same reasoning Rockstar has attached to most of its slips, and the same reasoning its fans find least comforting the second time.

Two consecutive delays is the single most important piece of context for anyone planning a purchase or a launch-week holiday. It is also why the phrase "confirmed release date" deserves care: what is confirmed is Rockstar's *current* plan, published on its own storefronts.

## Why this date looks harder to move than the last one

This is analysis, not an announcement. Four things are materially different from May 2026, a date that was still soft:

- **Money has changed hands.** Pre-orders opened on 25 June 2026 through the official stores, so a large base of buyers has already committed funds; the tiers and their prices are set out in [where to buy GTA 6 legitimately](/news/where-to-buy-gta-6-legitimately).
- **Commercial detail is published.** Two editions are listed on storefronts with real prices, which is the level of commitment a publisher does not normally print and then revoke.
- **A pre-load window has been attached to the date.** Reporting covered in our [installation guide](/news/how-to-install-gta-6) puts pre-loading for pre-order customers at 12 November 2026, exactly one week before launch. A pre-load date is infrastructure, not marketing.
- **The final phase of marketing is running.** Through the summer Rockstar moved from teasers to long-form gameplay showcases and live storefront promotion — the kind of expenditure a studio does not usually fund while a delay is under active consideration.

Taken together, those points raise confidence considerably. They do not make the date immune to slipping, and we will not pretend otherwise.
