## A frozen queue and a restarting queue are different faults

Both look identical in a screenshot and both end with you not playing, but they come apart immediately if you watch the entry for ten minutes. A number that never appears at all usually means the download is not actually running: it is paused, held behind another item, or waiting for a console that has gone to sleep without permission to keep working. A number that climbs and then collapses back means the transfer is running and something is killing it, which is a network or storage conversation rather than a settings conversation.

## Reading the queue

| What the entry does | First thing to check |
| --- | --- |
| Paused, no time estimate | Whether the console is permitted to download while resting, and whether the item was paused by hand |
| Queued behind another title | Queue priority; the other download is finishing first regardless of which one you care about |
| Creeps up, then resets to nothing | Connection stability, and available space during assembly |
| Shows a percentage, no movement | Free space that has just run out, then the store service |

## The hidden cause is nearly always the drive

A queue that stops mid-transfer with no useful message is often a full disk discovered during unpacking rather than at the start, because the listed download size and the space a completed install needs are not the same number. Check free space again before you touch the network. The rest of the pre-launch picture, including what to do when a licence rather than the pipe is the obstacle, is in [GTA 6 download and install errors](/news/gta-6-download-and-install-errors).

## Launch weekend, when the line is the problem

Consoles idle in rest mode only download if they are configured to stay connected, and the equivalent power state on the other platform is sleep rather than full shutdown; both are documented by [PlayStation support](https://en.support.playstation.com/en_gb/) and [Xbox support](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/). Beyond that, some networks shed heavy traffic during peak hours, and the peak hours of a release are exactly when everyone else is pulling the same file. Wired beats wireless for a transfer this size. The reliable workaround is not a faster connection but a quieter window: start the download at four in the morning, or start it weeks early if a pre-load ever opens. Note that on 23 September 2026 none of this can be happening, because no GTA 6 file is available to any console; if your PC is reporting a stalled GTA 6 download, it is downloading something that was never the game.
