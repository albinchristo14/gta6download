## Reaching the end of the hardware question early

Most of these pages are about whether a card is enough. This one is about what happens when that framing stops applying. The GeForce RTX 4080 Super is a 2024 ultra-tier product with 16 GB of memory — comfortably above every capacity argument being made anywhere in this catalog, and above the shared 16 GB pools inside the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X that will carry the game at launch on 19 November 2026.

On paper the interesting constraint has moved off the graphics card completely. In practice, the constraint that replaced it is more absolute: there is still no PC version of GTA 6, announced or otherwise, so nothing is being limited by anything.

## What "not the bottleneck" is secretly claiming

It is tempting to finish the thought and say a card this capable would not cap a hypothetical port. Notice what that sentence needs in order to be true. Bottlenecks are relationships, not properties — a graphics card becomes the limiting component only relative to a renderer, a resolution, a target image quality and a processor feeding it. All four of those are unwritten. The claim is not conservative; it is empty, just in a direction that happens to flatter your hardware.

There is a second emptiness underneath. Ports are frequently bounded by engine and tooling decisions long before they touch a card's ceiling, which is precisely why so many well-equipped PCs were once held back by poorly shipped conversions. Owning an ultra-tier GPU has historically been no guarantee of anything at all.

## Where an owner of this card should actually look

- A processor fast enough not to stall the simulation and streaming work a dense open world generates.
- Storage and memory outside the graphics card, which large modern games reach sooner than most people expect.
- A driver and upscaling stack that a future build would have to opt into — see [GTA 6 PC download status](/gta-6-download-pc) for what has been confirmed about PC support and what has not.

Anyone who tells you they have measured this card against the game is selling something; no downloadable GTA 6 exists, and every link purporting to offer one leads to malware or a fake installer.
