**There are no GTA 6 system requirements, official or otherwise, because Rockstar has not announced a PC version.** The game is confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on 19 November 2026. Every Windows spec table you have seen — the 16 GB of RAM, the RTX 3060, the 150 GB install — was written by a site that needed you to click something.

That is an unsatisfying answer to a real question, so below is the most that honest reasoning can produce: what we know from primary sources, what hardware the confirmed platforms actually contain, and which predictions are extrapolation rather than news.

## What is official, in three lines

- Two platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC, no previous-generation consoles, no cloud or streaming announcement.
- No published resolution or frame-rate target for either console. Store listings describe the game for the current generation; Rockstar has not committed to a number.
- No install size for any platform. Nothing on the official pages states storage requirements, so a "file size" figure anywhere else is not sourced.

## The only real requirements evidence is from GTA 5

The most useful published data point is Rockstar's own PC requirements for its previous game, which still stand as the last statement of how the studio approaches Windows hardware.

| GTA 5 (official, 2015) | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPU | Intel Core 2 Quad Q6600 / AMD Phenom 9850 | Intel Core i5 3470 / AMD FX-8350 |
| RAM | 4 GB | 8 GB |
| GPU | NVIDIA 9800 GT 1 GB / AMD HD 4870 1 GB | NVIDIA GTX 660 2 GB / AMD HD 7870 2 GB |

Two things in that table still predict GTA 6 behaviour, because they are properties of Rockstar's engine and launcher rather than of one release:

1. **Instruction-set requirements break otherwise-capable PCs.** Rockstar's own support guidance for its launcher-era PC games documents CPUs lacking a required instruction set failing to start the game at all — the classic GTA 5 case is a pre-2011 processor without AVX. A future GTA 6 will very likely have its own hard cut-off like this, and it will not be discoverable by benchmarking: the game simply will not launch.
2. **Rockstar's PC ports arrive late.** GTA 5 reached consoles in September 2013 and Windows in April 2015 — roughly eighteen months. That history is the reason "PC in 2028" circulates. It is precedent, not an announcement, and precedent has already been broken once: Red Dead Redemption 2 came to PC about a year after consoles.

## What the confirmed consoles tell us about a future PC build

Reasoning, clearly labelled as reasoning. PS5 and Xbox Series X are both around 10–12 TFLOPS of compute with 16 GB of shared memory and fast custom SSDs. A PC version has to ship to machines that a mainstream gaming PC in 2026 already exceeds, which is precisely why Rockstar can take its time: the install base is enormous, and a port is a separate engineering programme on an engine that has no console-generation hand-me-down problem.

From that you can defend a projection, not a requirement. If a PC version is announced on Rockstar's usual lag, the hardware it ships for will be the hardware people already own in 2027–2028, so an honest guess is: a modern 6-core CPU, 16 GB of RAM, and an 8 GB card roughly at RTX 3060 / RX 6600 XT class for 1080p. Treat that as a floor for the era, not as a Rockstar statement. It is also the reason our [FPS estimator](/tools/fps-estimator) labels every output as a projection.

## Storage is the question worth planning for

Even without a number, the shape of the problem is knowable. A game of this scale on a 1 TB console drive will be a large fraction of it, and both PS5 and Series S owners commonly run near-full drives. Two practical consequences, neither of which requires a leaked spec sheet:

- Free the space now rather than on 12 November, when [pre-load](/news/gta-6-preload-and-install-guide) is reported to begin and download queues are heaviest.
- An external USB drive is not a safe assumption for a current-generation game: both consoles restrict which titles run from external storage, and the internal SSD is the supported location.

## How to read the next announcement properly

When a PC version is confirmed, the requirement block will appear on the store page with a publisher, a price and a Windows version — and Rockstar's support site will list it in the same format it already uses for its existing PC games. Until a page like that exists, the only defensible statement about GTA 6 system requirements is that they do not exist yet, which is also the fastest way to spot a fake: any site that gives you a number today is guessing, and a site that gives you a download button is lying.
