**A video titled “GTA 6 mod menu” does not establish that a usable GTA 6 mod exists.** This guide explains what evidence a mod claim needs. It does not offer mod downloads, trainers, paid menus, or promises of online money.

## What is confirmed, and what is not established here?

At publication, Rockstar's GTA VI product page lists a **19 November 2026** release for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The page does not list a PC release or describe a modding system. That absence is not proof that modding will never be possible; it means this guide cannot treat future support as confirmed.

We have not verified a GTA 6 mod installation or a compatible mod menu. Until there is evidence for the exact game, version, and platform involved, treat a compatibility claim as unverified—not as an installation recommendation.

[Check the current official availability](/gta-6-download) before evaluating software that claims to modify the game.

## Mods, trainers, and built-in cheats are different

A **mod** changes or adds game content or behavior. A **trainer** is an external program intended to alter a running game. A **mod menu** is an interface for modifications; the name alone tells you nothing about how it works or whether it is permitted.

A **built-in cheat code** is a feature of the game itself, if the game supports it. It is not an external download. Our [GTA 6 cheat-code guide](/news/gta-6-cheat-codes) keeps that distinction clear rather than presenting a downloadable tool as a code.

## A GTA 6-themed GTA 5 mod is still for GTA 5

A creator might use another game to recreate a trailer, character, vehicle, or location associated with GTA 6. That can be a fan project without being a modification of GTA 6 itself.

Check the underlying game's name in the project description, not just the video title. A thumbnail showing a familiar character or a Vice City-style scene is not a compatibility test. Instructions, save files, or tools for GTA 5 should not be assumed to work in GTA 6.

## Evidence to look for before considering a future mod

1. **Exact target:** the game, platform, game build, and required dependencies are clearly identified.
2. **Reproducible behavior:** the creator explains what changes and how other people can independently check the result.
3. **Maintenance history:** version notes describe supported builds and known problems rather than promising universal compatibility.
4. **Permission and scope:** the project distinguishes offline modifications from online services and acknowledges applicable game and platform rules.
5. **Recovery information:** instructions explain how to preserve saves and undo changes without disabling security protections.

These checks help assess a claim; they do not certify a download as safe. A polished website or a claim of “undetectable” software is not a substitute for evidence.

## Keep online accounts and saves out of the experiment

Do not provide account passwords, session cookies, or payment details to a menu seller. Do not assume a single-player modification is permitted in an online mode. Consult the applicable terms before modifying anything, and avoid tools that promise to evade enforcement or alter other players' experience.

Before any legitimate, supported modification, preserve a separate backup of your saves and understand the restore process. No particular GTA 6 backup or mod-installation method is verified by this guide.
