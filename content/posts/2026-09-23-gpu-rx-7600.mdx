## The reader this page is written for

The Radeon RX 7600 is a mainstream AMD card from 2023 with 8 GB of video memory — the sort of part chosen by someone building on a budget, who researches carefully and expects the numbers to matter. That is exactly why this particular page needs to say something uncomfortable first. Value-segment buyers are the audience most aggressively targeted around a big release, because a purchase they cannot afford is being dangled in front of them by sites with a countdown clock and a green button.

GTA 6 arrives on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no announced PC version, and therefore no PC requirement list that any card, at any price, is being measured against.

## Eight gigabytes with no published bar

Within a graphics card, the memory budget is where textures, geometry and frame buffers physically live, which makes 8 GB the smallest figure in this catalog and a fair question for anyone planning a build. It is also a question that cannot be answered from thin air. Without a Windows release, an official preset quality, or a stated target resolution from Rockstar, an 8 GB card is not failing a specification or clearing one; the specification simply has not been drafted. Anyone who publishes a table for this card alongside GTA 6 has written it themselves.

## Where the pressure actually comes from

A countdown-timer page works on a small number of assumptions, and it is worth naming them out loud:

- That a file exists which you are about to miss, when no game file has been released to anybody.
- That urgency is a property of the release rather than of the site's advertising revenue.
- That a cheaper graphics card is the reason you are excluded, so the answer is a purchase or a workaround.
